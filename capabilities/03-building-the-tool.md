# Building the tool instead of waiting for it

Some operational problems are worth solving with a real tool, not a workaround, and don't need a full engineering team to get there.

At Le Wagon I built two AI agents to close specific operational gaps:

- One that analyzes mid-batch student surveys to surface issues while there's still time to act on them.
- One that checks invoice compliance across four countries, each with different tax rules and mandatory mentions.

Both came from the same reasoning: the problem was specific and recurring enough to justify a dedicated tool, the manual version was slow enough that people were already improvising workarounds, and building it myself was faster than waiting for a slot on someone else's roadmap.
