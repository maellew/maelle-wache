# Building a go-to-market from zero

Most GTM work starts with a category that already has a shape: competitors, a budget line, a job title the buyer recognizes. Building one from zero means none of that exists yet. The first job isn't messaging, it's figuring out who's even supposed to buy this and why they'd believe it matters.

In practice:

- More time on customer discovery than positioning, early on.
- The first pitch is a hypothesis to break, not a script to defend.
- The target buyer can change entirely if early conversations point somewhere else.

There's no existing playbook or qualifying process to inherit either, so GTM infrastructure gets built at the same time as GTM itself, based on what the first real conversations show.
