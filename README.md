**AI transformation, adoption & operations**

I help teams turn business priorities into practical tools and ways of working. My work connects strategy, people and execution: understanding real workflows, choosing where to act, building useful solutions and helping teams adopt them.

I'm currently Founding GTM & Operations Lead at Raison. My experience spans operational AI enablement, cross-functional transformation and building from zero.

## How I work

1. **Understand** the work, the people and the problem before choosing a tool.
2. **Prioritize** opportunities against value, feasibility and constraints.
3. **Build and test** practical solutions with the people who will use them.
4. **Enable adoption** through hands-on support, feedback and clear ownership.
5. **Measure and improve** using evidence that reflects the outcome, not just access.
