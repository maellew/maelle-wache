# Supporting invoice review with an AI assistant

**Le Wagon · AI and operations · Real work**

**At a glance** — I designed an assistant that prepares invoice checks and flags points requiring human attention, helping an operations team review a repetitive workflow.

## The problem

Operational teams needed to review invoices against requirements that varied with the context. The process involved repetitive checks while still requiring care and an accountable person to make the final decision.

## How I approached it

I started with the team's existing workflow and the checks it needed to perform. I configured an assistant to prepare a structured review and surface potential issues, then supported the team in using it and gathering feedback.

## The solution and what it unlocked

The assistant gave reviewers a structured first pass and made potential issues easier to find. People retained responsibility for reviewing the output and making decisions. It did not provide autonomous legal or tax advice. Detailed performance figures are omitted until their scope and supporting evidence can be confirmed.
