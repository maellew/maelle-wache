# Migrating a team to a new AI platform, mid-reorganization

*Real case, from two years at Le Wagon. No internal quotes or names.*

**Context**: operations teams running bootcamps across multiple concurrent programs, in two languages, moving from ad hoc AI tool use to a single company-wide platform (Dust), at the same time as a reorganization of those teams.

**Starting assumption**: the main task was technical, get people set up on the new tool and trained on how to use it.

**What changed my mind**: after rollout and training, usage stayed low outside a few early adopters. The tool worked. What was missing was a use case people recognized as theirs, and a way to see what already existed instead of rebuilding it from scratch each time.

**What I did**:

- Ran a hackathon for the operations teams to surface use cases from the people who'd use them, instead of defining use cases top-down.
- Organized a knowledge base so a solution built by one team could be found and reused by another.
- Supervised the change management side of the transition for teams going through a reorg at the same time.
- Worked to break down the silos between teams so a use case solved once didn't need solving again elsewhere.

**Result**: usage moved from a handful of early adopters to routine use across teams, on the use cases that matched something people were already doing. The knowledge base and the cross-team habit it created outlasted the migration itself.
