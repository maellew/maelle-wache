# Helping teams adopt AI in their daily work

**Le Wagon · AI adoption and learning · Real work**

**At a glance** — I worked with operational teams to identify useful AI applications, create opportunities to practise together and support colleagues individually as they integrated tools into their work.

## The problem

Access to an AI platform and general training did not, by themselves, ensure that people knew how to use it for the tasks they faced every day. Roles, workflows and levels of confidence varied across the team.

## How I approached it

I started with conversations about real tasks and friction points. I organised hands-on hackathons and sharing moments so colleagues could explore use cases together, and provided one-to-one support adapted to each person's work. I documented useful approaches and used feedback to improve the tools and guidance.

## The solution and what it unlocked

The approach gave colleagues a practical entry point through tasks they recognised, as well as opportunities to learn from one another and continue refining their use. It combined accessible training, shared practices and individual support. This account does not claim a measured adoption rate or a result across every team.
